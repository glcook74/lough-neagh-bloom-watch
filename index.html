Turning this into a real forecast
  • Feed the buoy readings into the model itself. They are shown beside it today, not used by it.
  • Check the satellite algae index against DAERA's water samples, so its colours can be trusted as amounts.
  • Add river flows, so the model knows how much water and phosphorus arrives after rain.
  • Check the model against what happened in the 2023 to 2026 blooms, then tune it until it would have called them.
Where the facts come from
  • Lough size, depth, shoreline and flushing time: Lough Neagh Partnership facts flyer.
  • Phosphorus by river and by source: AFBI modelling for the NEW Harmonica project, 2021 reference year, shared by DAERA in June 2025 and still under review at the time.
  • Zebra mussels, water clarity and sediment phosphorus: AFBI and DAERA statements, 2023 and 2024.
  • Live forecast: Met Office Weather DataHub, Global Spot three-hourly forecast for the centre of the lough.
  • Past 30 days of weather: Open-Meteo weather model archive (open-meteo.com), CC BY 4.0.
  • Buoy readings, confirmed blue-green algae locations and treatment works: DAERA open data, Open Government Licence v3.0. Buoy readings are provisional and not validated.
  • River phosphorus tests 1990 to 2024, water body status 2018 and NI Water storm overflows (May 2024): DAERA open data, Open Government Licence v3.0. The river test data is published raw and may contain errors.
  • Rivers tab: DAERA river water body outlines (2018), river phosphorus, ammonia and oxygen demand tests to 2024, NI Water works and overflows, pollution incidents, field risk scores and the 2019 farm census. Open Government Licence v3.0.
  • Key areas: the same DAERA river test, storm overflow and pollution incident data, ranked place by place. Storm overflow figures are NI Water model predictions, not measurements.
  • Likely sources: the DAERA datasets above matched to DAERA's Local Management Area outlines. Livestock is shared between basins by land area, which is an estimate.
  • Land cover: Corine Land Cover 2018, Copernicus Land Monitoring Service, European Environment Agency.
  • Slurry spreading dates and rules: DAERA Nutrients Action Programme guidance.
  • Basin outlines, the 2019 farm census, pollution incidents 2011 to 2024 and field risk scores: DAERA open data, Open Government Licence v3.0. The farm census is a National Statistics publication, © Crown Copyright 2021.
  • Satellite by date and algae from space: Sentinel-2 images from the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem, processed with Sentinel Hub. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data. The algae index is the Normalised Difference Chlorophyll Index.
  • Satellite tab: the same Sentinel-2 images, summarised with the Sentinel Hub Statistical API for nine boxes over the lough at about 115 m detail, every pass since 2023 with under 70% cloud.
  • Investigate tab: weather and basin rainfall from the Open-Meteo ERA5 archive (open-meteo.com, CC BY 4.0), buoy readings from DAERA open data, and the Sentinel-2 algae index. Basin rainfall is taken at one point near the middle of each basin.
  • Forecast tab: a weather-only score built from the Open-Meteo ERA5 archive, tuned and tested against the Sentinel-2 algae index. It is a research check, not an operational forecast.
  • Sharp aerial view: Esri World Imagery (Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS user community). Check Esri's terms before any commercial use.
  • Satellite 2024 view: Sentinel-2 cloudless 2024 (s2maps.eu) by EOX IT Services GmbH, contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2024, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for non-commercial use.
  • Daily photos: NASA Terra MODIS, through NASA EOSDIS GIBS.
  • Geology layers: Geological Survey of Northern Ireland 1:250,000 mapping. Contains Geological Survey of Northern Ireland materials © Crown Copyright, under the Open Government Licence.
  • Base map: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Markers for rivers, shores and places are positioned approximately.
