Turning this into a real forecast

  • Add Met Office observations for the past 30 days. The forecast for the next 5 days is already live.
  • Feed in real water readings. DAERA has monitoring buoys at Rea's Wood and Washing Bay and gauges on the six main rivers.
  • Add satellite images of the lough surface to see where blooms are today.
  • Check the model against what happened in the 2023 to 2026 blooms, then tune it until it would have called them.

Where the facts come from

  • Lough size, depth, shoreline and flushing time: Lough Neagh Partnership facts flyer.
  • Phosphorus by river and by source: AFBI modelling for the NEW Harmonica project, 2021 reference year, shared by DAERA in June 2025 and still under review at the time.
  • Zebra mussels, water clarity and sediment phosphorus: AFBI and DAERA statements, 2023 and 2024.
  • Live forecast: Met Office Weather DataHub, Global Spot three-hourly forecast for the centre of the lough.
  • Geology and depth bands on the map are simplified sketches.
